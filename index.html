    Who is this initiative for?+

    The business case targets individuals and organizations across residential, commercial, government, industrial and agricultural sectors in Abu Dhabi, Al Ain and Al Dhafra. Final eligibility rules will be published after approval.

    What will the awards recognize?+

    Electricity and water savings, practical implementation, effective use of resources, creativity, outreach, collaboration and social impact. Criteria and weightings will be tailored to each approved award.

    Are all 25 awards available now?+

    No. The business case proposes a phased rollout across 2026-2030. The 25 award types are preliminary and will be evaluated, prioritized and finalized during programme design.

    What supporting documents should I prepare?+

    Consider consumption records, before-and-after comparisons, project reports, calculations and photographs. Final required documents, templates and submission dates have not yet been confirmed.

    Does this website submit my application?+

    This is an interactive design preview. Drafts, demo applications and attachments stay in this browser on this device. No data is sent to TAQA. A secure submission service and approved privacy notice are required before launch.

      Storage & privacy

      This prototype does not transmit form data or attachments. Clicking Save draft or Save demo application stores your information and actual files in this browser's IndexedDB storage.

      Anyone with access to this browser profile may be able to access that data. Use a private device and avoid sensitive information. Browser cleanup can remove saved applications; a downloaded summary contains text and attachment names, not the attachment files.

      Delete stored drafts and demo applications with the button below. This does not delete summaries you downloaded. A production service requires an approved privacy notice, secure file handling and retention policy.
